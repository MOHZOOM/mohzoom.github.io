Featured case study · EMPHNET

From a workshop recording to a briefing leadership could act on

During my internship at EMPHNET, I was given the recording and transcript of a monthly workshop on Jordan's Family Health Team pilot, a national reform of primary healthcare. The ask: make it usable for the people who weren't in the room.

Meeting notes usually just retell what was said. I wanted something a manager could open five minutes before the next review and know exactly what's late, who owns it, and what might go wrong.

6page executive briefing
8actions with owner & deadline
5open risks flagged
4days, meeting to delivery
What I did
Broke the session into four themes, pulled every decision into an action register with owners and deadlines, and built a key-dates timeline for the next review.
The insight
One doctor was blocked from her final exam by a software filter. The meeting treated it as one case. I flagged that the cause was a system setting, so other specialists would likely hit the same wall before the deadline.
Why it matters
That's process thinking in practice: find the root cause, not just the symptom, and make the follow-up trackable.

The briefing is internal, so I only show cropped pages here, with names removed. Happy to walk through it in an interview.

Built alongside my EMPHNET work · 2026

Outbreak Watch

At EMPHNET, keeping up with outbreak news meant checking WHO, ECDC, CDC and others one site at a time. So I built a small site that does it automatically: it collects official reports every 30 minutes, checks every link and date on the real page, sorts reports by disease, country and category, and keeps a searchable archive.

  • 9 official sources, including WHO Disease Outbreak News, ECDC, US CDC, UKHSA and WHO AFRO
  • Every link is verified before saving, and duplicates are removed by URL and title
  • AI writes short neutral summaries, using only facts from the source and writing "Unknown" rather than guessing
  • Source-first: every item links back to the original report, and the site never makes up sample news
Python · Flask · SQLite · ScrapeGraphAI · OpenAI · Docker
